1. Scope of Services

These terms apply to seasonal residential snow removal services provided by Greenworks Construction & Companies Inc., operating as Greenworks Landscaping. Unless your quote states otherwise, each clearing visit includes:

  • Snow plowing and clearing of the driveway.
  • Shoveling and snow removal from the front walkway, steps, and main entrance.
  • Application of salt or ice melt to cleared surfaces to help prevent icy buildup.
  • Clearing of the windrow (the ridge of snow left across the driveway entrance by municipal plows) on our next pass after the city plow has gone by.
  • Monitoring and automatic service after every qualifying snowfall. No call needed.

Only the areas and services listed on your quote are included. Decks, patios, roofs, and hauling snow off site are not included unless specifically quoted.

2. Season and Term

The service season runs from November 15 to April 15 unless your quote states different dates. Snowfalls outside these dates are not included, but can usually be serviced on request at an additional per-visit charge.

3. Service Trigger and Timing

  • Service visits occur when snow accumulation reaches 5 cm (2 inches). Smaller accumulations may be left to melt or combined with the next event.
  • Properties are cleared within 12 hours after the snowfall ends, and usually much sooner. Routes run in a set order, so we cannot guarantee a specific arrival time.
  • During prolonged or heavy storms (roughly 15 cm or more), we may make an interim pass to keep the driveway usable and return for a final cleanup pass after the snow stops. Continuous or instant clearing during an active storm is not guaranteed.

4. Salting and Ice-Only Events

Salt or ice melt is applied to cleared surfaces as part of each clearing visit. Stand-alone ice events (freezing rain or refreeze with no plowable snow) are not included in the seasonal price and can be serviced on request at an additional charge per application.

5. Payment Terms

  • A 50% deposit is due on acceptance of this agreement. Service begins once the deposit is received.
  • The remaining balance is due by December 1. Service may be suspended if the balance remains unpaid after December 1, and resumes once the account is settled.
  • Overdue balances may be charged a late fee of 2% per month.
  • This is a fixed seasonal price. It does not change with the number of snowfalls, and no refunds or credits are given for mild winters.
  • All prices are subject to HST.

6. Property Condition and Damage

  • Greenworks Landscaping is not responsible for pre-existing damage to the driveway, curbs, walkways, or lawn edges. We may take date-stamped photos of the property before the first visit for both parties' records.
  • Minor surface scuffing from plow blades, shovels, or snowblowers, and minor lawn-edge marks along the driveway, are a normal part of snow removal and are not compensable. Lawn edge marks are repaired with topsoil and seed in spring where applicable.
  • The client must tell us before the season starts if the driveway is stamped, patterned, sealed, or freshly paved (asphalt under 1 year old), as these surfaces need modified equipment or methods.
  • The client must clearly mark raised obstacles near cleared areas, such as landscape lighting, edging, sprinkler heads, and water shut-off valves. Damage to unmarked obstacles hidden by snow is the client's responsibility.
  • Any claim of damage caused by our equipment must be reported within 48 hours of the service visit so we can inspect and address it promptly.

7. Client Responsibilities

  • Keep vehicles off the driveway on snow days where possible. Areas blocked by vehicles, bins, basketball nets, or other obstacles will be cleared around, and we do not return to clear missed spots once the vehicle moves unless an extra visit is arranged.
  • Keep cords, hoses, toys, newspapers, and similar items out of the snow-clearing areas. We are not responsible for items hidden under snow.
  • Keep pets indoors while the crew is working.

8. Ice, Slips, and Liability

  • We clear and treat surfaces according to the trigger and timing in Section 3. Between visits, melting and refreezing can create slippery conditions quickly. The client remains responsible for monitoring and maintaining safe conditions on their property between visits.
  • Greenworks Landscaping carries liability insurance.
  • In any claim arising from this agreement, the total liability of either party, regardless of the form of action, is limited to the total seasonal contract amount.

9. Exclusions

  • Packed snow or ice existing before the first visit of the season.
  • Drifting or wind-blown snow when fresh snowfall has not reached the 5 cm trigger.
  • Snow pushed onto the property by third parties after our visit.
  • Roofs, decks, patios, and hauling snow away from the property, unless quoted separately.

10. Emergency and Extra Services

Emergency clearing, extra visits, and clearing outside the quoted areas are available on request and are billed in addition to the seasonal price at rates confirmed before the work is done.

11. Cancellation

The client may cancel before the season starts (November 15) for a full refund of any deposit paid. After the season begins, cancellations receive a prorated credit for full unserviced months, less the deposit. Greenworks Landscaping may suspend or end service for non-payment as described in Section 5.
