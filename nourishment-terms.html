7. Seeding, Germination and Expected Results

Grass seed is a living product and its performance depends on watering, weather, soil conditions, and the state of the existing lawn. For these reasons:

  • Germination and final results are not guaranteed. New seed typically germinates in 7 to 21 days, and visible thickening develops over the following weeks.
  • Overseeding and slit seeding improve an existing lawn gradually. They do not produce an instant new lawn, and results vary across different areas of the property.
  • Where seeded areas fail primarily because the recommended watering and aftercare in Section 8 were not followed, any reseeding will be quoted as a separate chargeable service.
  • Soil cores left on the lawn after core aeration are intentional. They break down naturally within one to two weeks and return nutrients to the soil.

8. Client Aftercare Responsibilities (Seeded Areas)

  • Water seeded areas lightly once or twice daily for the first 2 to 3 weeks, keeping the top layer of soil consistently moist (skip days with adequate rainfall).
  • Avoid mowing newly seeded areas until the new grass reaches roughly 3 inches, typically 2 to 4 weeks after service.
  • Keep foot traffic and pets off seeded areas as much as possible during establishment.
  • Avoid applying weed control products to newly seeded areas for 4 to 6 weeks after seeding.
