The Right Service at the Right Time

Timing matters as much as technique. Here's how we plan renovation work through the year in Ontario.

Fall Renovation

Late August to October

Fall is the single best time to renovate a cool-season lawn: warm soil, cooler air, fewer weeds, and a full season for roots to establish before summer stress.

  • Dethatching / power raking
  • Core aeration
  • Overseeding & bare spot repair
  • Top dressing with seeding

Spring Renovation

April to Early June

Spring work wakes the lawn up and repairs winter damage (snow mold, salt burn and plow scars) so your lawn starts the season strong.

  • Liquid aeration (gentle on soft spring soil)
  • Light overseeding & patch repair
  • Top dressing to level winter damage
  • Starter fertilization for new growth

Rebuild a Thicker, Healthier Lawn

Compacted soil, thatch buildup and thin, patchy grass don't fix themselves. They get worse every season. Our renovation services work together as a system: we clear out the dead layer, open up the soil, feed it, and reseed it so new grass can take root and thrive. Done in the right order, at the right time of year, the results are dramatic.

  • Remove the Thatch Layer Power raking strips out the dead, matted layer that blocks water, air and nutrients from ever reaching the roots.
  • Open Up Compacted Soil Core or liquid aeration lets your lawn breathe again, so water and nutrients penetrate deep instead of running off.
  • Reseed & Fill Bare Spots Premium seed blends chosen for the Ontario climate fill in thin and bare areas and crowd out weeds naturally.
  • Feed With Top Dressing A fine layer of quality soil/compost worked in with fresh seed levels the lawn and gives new grass the best possible start.
