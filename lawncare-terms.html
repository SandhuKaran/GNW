1. Services & Scheduling

All service schedules are subject to change due to weather conditions, statutory holidays, or unforeseen circumstances. While we strive to maintain a consistent weekly/ bi-weekly or monthly route, Greenworks Landscaping reserves the right to adjust service days to ensure the safety of our crew and the quality of the work.

2. Payment Terms & Amortized Billing

  • Invoicing: Invoices are generated as agreed upon in the accepted quote. Payment is due upon receipt unless otherwise specified.
  • Amortized/Split Payments: If the client has opted for an amortized billing schedule (e.g., a 5-month or 7-month split), the client agrees to fulfill the entire payment schedule for the total value of the accepted quote, even if the seasonal services (e.g., aeration, cleanups) are completed before the payment schedule concludes.
  • Late Fees: Outstanding balances past 15 days may be subject to a late fee of 2% per month.
  • Taxes: All services are subject to HST.

3. Quote Accuracy & Lot Sizing

All estimates and quotes generated online or over the phone are based on the estimated lot size tier selected by the client (e.g., Up to 1,000 sq ft; Standard 1,001 - 3,000 sq ft; etc.). Greenworks Landscaping reserves the right to verify the property measurements upon our first site visit. If the actual serviceable lawn area is found to be larger than the size tier initially selected, we will notify the client immediately, and the quote and/or monthly billing plan will be adjusted to accurately reflect the correct property size tier before further services continue.

4. Property Access & Client Responsibilities

To ensure safe and efficient service, the client agrees to:

  • Clear the lawn of all toys, hoses, furniture, and pet waste prior to the scheduled service day. Greenworks Landscaping reserves the right to skip areas cluttered with debris or charge an additional cleanup fee.
  • Ensure all access gates are unlocked on the scheduled day of service. If a gate is locked and the backyard is inaccessible, the crew will service the accessible areas, and the client will be billed for the standard visit.
  • Keep all pets indoors while our crew is actively working on the property.

5. Damages & Liability

  • Underground Hazards: Greenworks Landscaping is not responsible for damage to unmarked underground systems, including but not limited to sprinkler heads, irrigation lines, invisible dog fences, landscape lighting cables, or shallow utility wires. It is the client's responsibility to clearly flag these hazards prior to services such as Core Aeration or Dethatching.
  • Property Damage: We take great care working around your property. In the unlikely event of damage caused directly by our equipment to siding, windows, or fencing, please report it to us within 24 hours of the service so we can address it promptly.
  • Limitation of Liability: In any legal action arising from this agreement, the total liability of either party for damages, regardless of the form of action, shall be strictly limited to the total monetary amount of this contract.

6. Weed Control & Treatment Notifications

  • Service Confirmation: Once a weed control or fertilizer application has been successfully completed on your property, our team will place a standard safety sign on your lawn.
  • App Notification: You will also receive an instant notification through our client app confirming that the treatment was applied.
  • Safety Protocol: Please follow all instructions provided on the physical lawn sign regarding safe re-entry times for children and pets to ensure the product dries and settles properly.

7. Cancellations & Pauses

  • Service Pauses: If you need to skip a scheduled mowing visit (e.g., due to drought or vacation), please provide at least 48 hours' notice.
  • Cancellation: This agreement may be cancelled by either party at any time with 14 days' written notice. Upon cancellation, any unbilled services that have already been performed, or the remaining balance of any completed amortized services, will be invoiced and become due immediately.

8. Satisfaction Guarantee

Your satisfaction is our priority. If you are unhappy with the quality of a specific service, please contact us within 24 hours. We will gladly return to correct the issue at no additional charge.
