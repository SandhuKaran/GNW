Questions

Snow Removal FAQ

Do I need to call you when it snows?

No. Once you are on our seasonal route, we monitor the weather and come automatically after every qualifying snowfall of 5 cm (2 inches) or more.

What areas do you serve?

We provide snow removal in Halton Hills, including Georgetown and Acton, and the GTA. Not sure if you are in our area? Send a quote request or give us a call.

How will I know my property has been cleared?

You get a notification on our client app after each visit, so you know your driveway and walkways are done without having to look outside.

How fast will my property be cleared?

Residential properties are cleared within 12 hours after the snowfall ends, and usually much sooner. During heavy storms we may make an interim pass to keep your driveway usable, then return for a final cleanup. Commercial and industrial timing is agreed per site.

Is salting included?

Yes. Salt or ice melt is applied to cleared surfaces on each visit. If you would rather not have salt on your property, let us know and your quote will say it is not included.

What if we get a mild winter?

Residential contracts are a fixed seasonal price, so your cost stays the same whether we get five storms or fifty. You always know exactly what you will pay.

Can I sign up after the season has started?

Often, yes, depending on space on our route. Send a quote request or give us a call and we will let you know right away.

How is commercial and industrial snow removal priced?

Every site is different, so we quote each one individually based on lot size, walkways, docks, hours of operation, and how quickly areas need to be open. Tell us about your site and we will arrange a visit if needed.

Where does the snow go?

Cleared snow is piled on site, usually along driveway and lot edges. If space runs out, snow hauling is available at an additional charge.
